# How to export your chats

Draft guides for agents. These steps change when the apps update; check them before showing them in the app.

## Instagram
1. Open Instagram → your profile → menu → **Settings and privacy**.
2. **Your activity** (or Accounts Center) → **Download your information**.
3. Choose **Some of your information** → tick **Messages**.
4. Choose **Download to device**, set **Format: JSON**, date range **All time**.
5. Submit. Instagram emails you when the file is ready (hours to days). Download the ZIP within 4 days and drop it into the app.

## Facebook Messenger
1. Facebook → **Settings and privacy** → **Settings** → **Accounts Center** → **Your information and permissions** → **Download your information**.
2. Select **Messages**, **Format: JSON**, **All time**.
3. Download the ZIP when emailed and drop it into the app.

## WhatsApp
1. Open the chat with the lead.
2. Android: ⋮ → **More** → **Export chat**. iPhone: tap the contact name → **Export chat**.
3. Choose **Without media** (smaller and faster).
4. Save or share the `.txt` (or `.zip`) and drop it into the app. Repeat per chat.
- Using the WhatsApp **Business** app? A direct connection (no exports) is planned.

## LinkedIn
1. **Me** → **Settings & Privacy** → **Data privacy** → **Get a copy of your data**.
2. Select **Messages** (or the full archive).
3. LinkedIn emails a link when ready. Download the ZIP and drop it into the app.
