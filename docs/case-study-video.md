# Case study: "Can AI Save His Failing Business?" (YouTube)

Summary written from the video transcript (the transcript itself is not stored here). Figures are the people's own claims in the video, not verified. The episode was sponsored by Hyper Agent, the tool they built on.

## The situation
An event business had 17 of 72 spots sold. The owner thought he needed more reach. The team found the money was in people who already knew him, had said they wanted to come, and never got a follow-up. Claim in the video: warm contacts close at 60 to 70%, cold ones at 5 to 20%.

## What they did
1. Exported his Instagram conversations from Meta.
2. AI read every conversation and graded each person on interest, confidence and relevance to the event, with a short summary.
3. A "top 5 to contact right now" list went to his phone, each with a suggested message. One was a person who had already said yes and only needed a payment follow-up; the owner had forgotten him (he gets 20 to 100 DMs a day).
4. AI read 19 recorded sales calls to describe who buys.
5. They reworked the offer to add real urgency. Their words: the machine found the leads but could not make them buy.
6. A dashboard showed leads by source and the pipeline; the owner said visibility removed his anxiety.
7. A "DM a keyword, get the booking link" story; over 100 people started overnight, few finished.
8. They handed the system over so he could run it alone.

Claimed result: $10K on day 3, about $180K after the team left.

## What we take
- Same export route as our plan (Meta export), which confirms it works.
- Grade each lead on interest and likelihood, with a short summary and the reason.
- A small daily Top 5 on the phone, not just a dashboard.
- Spot leads who already said yes and only need a nudge.
- Outcome tracking so the agent sees the money that came back.

## What we do not take
- **Offer-led reactivation.** Their team built a full AI system around one business, so it knew his offers. Our app only knows the uploaded conversations, so the offer stays with the agent. The app gives the agent questions to think about, from the lead's own words (founder decision, 19 Oct 2026).
- A one-off custom build on a general AI workspace. We are building one product for many agents.
